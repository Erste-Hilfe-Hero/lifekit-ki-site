# Closed beta tester instructions

Please use a fresh install when possible and record your device/OS/network in `device-results.csv`.

Required test path per device:

1. Cold launch and account login/guest creation.
2. Enter a realm and play combat for at least 10 minutes.
3. Loot items, open inventory, equip/swap items and use one ability repeatedly.
4. Enter and complete a dungeon; verify the dungeon boss reward is awarded once.
5. Suspend the app for at least 60 seconds, resume and verify the same account/character state.
6. Force-close during normal play, relaunch and reconnect.
7. Switch Wi-Fi ↔ cellular when the device supports it.
8. Perform one trade with another beta account.
9. Open the Black Counter/shop. On designated purchase-test accounts, complete the assigned sandbox/license purchase scenario only.
10. Verify Settings, language selection and the legal/support links.
11. Keep one continuous session open for at least 30 minutes.
12. Report crashes, ANRs, black screens, stuck loading, lost items, duplicate items, incorrect purchase state or unrecoverable disconnects immediately.

Do not use production payment credentials outside the official TestFlight / Google Play test environment.
