# Support and moderation runbook

- Every mute/ban/report decision requires an operator identity and reason and is written to the admin audit log.
- Never modify inventories manually without a ticket/reference and before/after audit evidence.
- Lost-item claims are investigated from persisted receipts/economy ledger; do not grant compensation when a duplicate receipt is already committed.
- Account deletion requests use the in-app path first; support verifies completion but never asks for passwords or recovery codes.
- Ban appeals are handled by a different reviewer where possible.
- Security/exploit reports are preserved separately from ordinary moderation reports and escalated before disclosure.
- Support targets and retention periods must match the published privacy policy before production launch.
